FlowSim supply chain

Stock-point performance

Measures use review periods after warm-up. Stock and backorders are end-of-period values. The fill rate is the share of each period's new demand filled from stock in that period. The bullwhip ratio is the variance of orders placed divided by the variance of orders received.

Orders up the chain

Each line is the quantity a stage ordered in each period, beside consumer demand. Wider swings upstream are the bullwhip effect. The dashed line marks the end of warm-up, and the vertical scale fits the measured weeks, so double-click the chart to include startup orders.

Stock, backorders, and orders

Values are recorded after each review's order. Orders received are consumer demand at the first stage and the downstream stage's orders elsewhere.

Raw records

The inventory records hold one row per stock point and period. A shipment with no arrival time was still in transit at the end.
